dea: your design-engineering team, on your own computer

AI specialists that know your brand, build mobile first, and check their own work, without handing over the keys to your projects. Runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.

Most AI tools are generalists: they write code fast and forget your brand guidelines just as fast. dea is built for designers who ship. Load your brand system once, and a team of specialist agents designs, builds, reviews and polishes against it, every time.

One chat. A whole studio behind it.

Ask for what you need. A lead agent plans the work and hands it to the right specialist:

UI engineer: responsive, mobile-first, accessible front ends in HTML, Tailwind and React
Design critic: hierarchy, usability and accessibility reviews before anything goes to a client
Brand strategist: positioning, naming, palettes and type direction
Arabic copywriter: professional Modern Standard Arabic, RTL-ready
Icon designer: icons drawn on your brand's grid and stroke rules
Design-tool specialist: works directly in your open design file through MCP
Security and deployment specialists: audits, headers and launch checklists
Your brand, enforced, not just remembered

Load a brand pack (tokens, typography, components, rules) and dea follows it. Built-in checkers verify every result for off-brand colours, fonts, contrast, mobile-first layout and RTL, before the agent calls the job done. It even translates print brand systems into web-ready design tokens.
