# Northwind site (example project)

**Brand:** northwind

A fictional project that shows how a brief works. Copy it for your own projects: one file per project, named after the project.

## Goal
A mobile-first product site for Northwind Outfitters: home, a product page per pack, and a contact page.

## Stack
HTML + Tailwind CSS v4 to start; React later. Hosted on Vercel.

## Audience and tone
Hikers and commuters who buy once and keep for years. Plain, specific copy with real numbers and units.

## Content notes
- Products: Trail Pack 20, Trail Pack 30, Trail Pack 45 (capacities in litres).
- Unknown specs stay "—" until the product team supplies them.
